Groups vs. Periods Trick

Memory Trick: How do you remember which way Groups and Periods go on the Periodic Table?

G.U.P & P.H

Groups go Up and down (Vertical Columns).
Periods go Horizontally (Rows across).

Phase Changes (Sublimation & Deposition)

Memory Trick: How do you remember the direct skip between Solid and Gas?

S.G.S ➔ Solid to Gas = Sublimation

Sublimation: Solid ➔ Gas (e.g., Dry Ice).
Deposition: Gas ➔ Solid (Depositing frost onto cold glass).

Cations vs. Anions (Charge & Loss/Gain)

Memory Trick: How do you remember which ion is positive/negative and whether electrons are lost or gained?

CA+ION is Paws-itive 🐱 & A-N-I-O-N = A Negative Ion 🧅

Cation (+) : Cation has a + sign. Metals LOSE electrons to become positive.
Anion (-) : A Negative Ion. Non-metals GAIN electrons to become negative.

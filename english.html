Active vs. Passive Voice Trick

Memory Trick: How do you quickly tell if a sentence is written in the Passive Voice?

Add "...by Zombies" 🧟‍♂️ after the verb!

If you can insert "by zombies" after the action verb and it still makes grammatical sense, it’s Passive Voice!
Example: "The homework was eaten [by zombies]" ➔ Passive!

Coordinating Conjunctions (FANBOYS)

Memory Trick: What are the 7 main coordinating conjunctions used to join two clauses?

F.A.N.B.O.Y.S

For • And • Nor • But • Or • Yet • So
Rule: Use a comma before a FANBOYS word when linking two independent sentences!

"Its" vs. "It's" Confusing Words

Memory Trick: How do you know when to use the apostrophe in "It's"?

Apostrophe = Short for "IS" or "HAS"

It's: Means "It is" or "It has" (e.g., It's raining = It is raining).
Its: Shows possession (e.g., The cat chased its tail).
